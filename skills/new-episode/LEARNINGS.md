# Learnings

What `new-episode` runs have taught, distilled from their `run-log.md` entries and the run event logs (`npm run episode:query`, `docs/episodes/runs.csv`). Each lesson is a change to the template pipeline, a reference or `SKILL.md`; when one lands, its entry here says where it went. Read this before tailoring a pipeline.

## Lessons

Each lesson names its evidence in the Run it came from and the change it proposes. The `pipeline-feedback` skill deletes implemented, discarded and stale lessons and renumbers the rest, since the formatter keeps an ordered list consecutive; cite a lesson by its title, not its number.

13. **A shared part was drawn two ways.** `CashflowBoard`, drawn on Slides 1, 6 and 10, had one interface but two drawings (surface, tints, signs, legend) until the Visual language named one Option as the reference drawing (`write-spec#4`). Proposed fix: `write-spec.md` step 1 says a part used by several Slides gets a reference drawing in the Visual language, and its designers draw only that.
14. **The Visual language bound Options the user had not picked.** After the lock, the Visual language was rewritten to the user's pick 1c, and two reviews failed the unpicked 1a, 1b, 6b, 10b and 10c against it (`write-spec#7`, `#8`). Proposed fix: `write-spec.md`'s Lock step says a Visual language change made at the Lock binds only picked Options, and the reviewer of a redesign judges only those.
15. **Locking left stale recommendations.** A locked Slide keeps the designer's `data-recommended` and a caption that argues against the user's pick (1a over 1c, 2a over 2c). Proposed fix: at the Lock, the picked Option's caption names the user's pick, and the staging test or the Spec contract decides whether `data-recommended` follows the pick.
16. **A note on one Slide was an Episode-wide change.** the user's "use €" sat on Slide 1, but the currency appears on three boards and in the Voice script. The orchestrator applied it everywhere, the Dossier included. Proposed fix: `write-spec.md`'s Lock step names Episode-wide notes (currency, a term, a tone), which go into the Visual language or the words of every Slide and are listed in the run-log entry.
17. **Two talk pages became the Spec's exemplars.** the user named `maintaining-markdown-for-ai` and `ai-token-economy` in `coaching-content` as the richness to aim for. A fork captured their 29 sections as images and catalogued them by claim type, with 14 proposed changes, among them the user's Spec page UX (pick by tab, note as a speech bubble, a full-width submit, a visual intro): `skills/new-episode/assets/exemplars/catalogue.md`. Open for the user: the best exemplar (`ai-token-economy/13`), the model for a recurring drawing, and title emphasis by weight or a new token.
18. **A Context marker beside a label swallowed the whole Slide's clicks.** In `onboard-ai-every-time`, the BRIEF × PRICE board and the venue pipeline placed markers with `className="static"`, as `ContextMarker`'s comment advised. That stripped the button's `relative`, so its `after:absolute after:-inset-1.5` hit area spanned the Slide: every click opened the last row's note and highlighted that row, and the 6 px inset gave the page a horizontal scroll (the user, feedback round 2). It had happened before; `never-too-early-sales-run-3` carries ten such markers. Fixed in the run branch: `ContextMarker` gains `placement="beside"` through the kit's `Marker`, and a caller's `static` maps to it. Proposed fix: the implementor references and the reviewer rubric name `placement="beside"` for a marker next to a label, and the visual check clicks a point away from every marker and asserts no Context drawer opens and the page's `scrollWidth` equals its `clientWidth`.
19. **Scene text keyed under `notes.*`.** In `never-too-early-sales-run-3`, the Spec's build notes keyed Scene text (Slide 2's board, Slide 7's cards, Slides 4 to 6) under `notes.*`, which FE-010 §3.1 keeps for Speaker notes; three implementors renamed it and a review failed on it (run log ~425, 489, 523). Proposed fix: `write-spec.md`'s build-notes line keys Scene text after what it shows (`board.*`, `cards.*`, `lanes.*`, `deals.*`), and the figcaption rubric checks it.
20. **The Scene text standards reached implementors only after a failed Attempt.** Section 4's Attempt 1 failed FE-011 §3.4 (`<b>` at 700) and Section 2 failed FE-010; Section 3 passed only because the orchestrator relayed both lessons by hand (Inputs of `implement-section-how-to-start-doing-sales`). Proposed fix: `implement-section.md` lists the Scene text standards up front: semibold `<b>`, `notes.*` reserved, `div > dt + dd`, no `t.rich` `b` override.
21. **Speaker notes repeated the Voice script.** `write-voice-script` failed rubric line 5 twice before Attempt 3 deleted the repeating note; the "talking points" Score failed three times (`write-dossier` Notes ~214–228). Proposed fix: `write-dossier.md` says a Speaker note carries only what the speaker leaves out, and the writer compares each note header with the Slide's Voice items before handing on.
22. **One client industry per Slide was caught by the user, not the reviewer.** The rubric line existed, yet all three round-3 items were mixed industries, a bakery beside a dental practice among them (`feedback/03`). Proposed fix: `write-spec.md` step 1 has the designer name each Slide's industry in the Visual language, and the reviewer checks every mock for it before the gate.
23. **Slide defaults arrived as per-Slide feedback.** Rounds 2 and 4 asked 13 times for what every Slide should have: the shared `SlideHeader`, the computed Eyebrow, the once-only entrance, the shared hover classes, a section slide of at least 40svh, and a Context marker beside every sourced fact (`feedback/02` items 3–9, `feedback/04` items 6–12). Proposed fix: `implement-section.md` lists them as standing defaults, and the reviewer rubric checks each.
24. **A shared part's variants grew Section by Section.** `CashflowBoard` gained `variant: 'curve'` and `ring` in Section 3, then `labelGap` and `stop: 'beside'` in Section 5 after its Attempt 1 failed; lesson 13 gives a shared part a drawing, not its full prop surface. Proposed fix: the Visual language lists every variant the Options need before the Lock, and `implement-section.md` builds a shared part's first user first.
25. **Run-level Needs human buried the PR checklist.** Every implement job repeated "the German text is the implementor's translation" and "interactive pass skipped, no browser", and `open-pr` collected 46 items into `## Needs Han`. Proposed fix: `implement-section.md` and `open-pr.md` list run-level facts once in the PR body, and a job logs Needs human only for a new case.
26. **The Brief reviewer failed seven sections of a Brief the user approved unedited.** In `onboard-ai-every-time`, the `brief-grill` reviewer failed the Audience, the Style, the story rules and the research questions on the only Brief draft (events seq 18, 21, 22, 31, 34, 36, 39); the gate commit changed only the human verified mark, and no later job used the failures. One Run is weak evidence, since `onboard-ai-like-a-colleague` passed first time. Proposed fix: at the next Promotion, compare the failed checks with the approved Brief in `brief-grill.md`; loosen a check that a correct Brief fails and keep one that found a real gap.

Gate diffs: The user changed only `status:` in the Brief and Manuscript gate pairs, and the pipeline pair mixes their approval with the template merge. No correction recurs, so no rubric line comes from them yet.
