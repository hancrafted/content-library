Before you approve

  • Accessibility: does every Option keep its contrast, focus order and motion in check?
  • German: does the text still fit when it runs about a third longer?
  • Build cost: what does each pick cost, and is it worth it?
  • Reuse: what does an Option copy from a shared Slide, and will the copies drift?
