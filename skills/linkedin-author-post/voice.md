# LinkedIn voice

What a LinkedIn post adds to `docs/profile/tone-guide.md`, which holds Han's voice itself. Every rule here binds every draft. The learn branch appends to the last two sections; each learned line names the post it came from.

## Rules

1. **The hook carries the post.** LinkedIn shows a line or two before "…more", so the first sentence promises the value the post delivers.
2. **Value before announcement.** The insight, number or problem comes first; the Event, Episode or Explorable it comes from follows.
3. **120 to 180 words**, in short paragraphs, for an Event or release post. An offering post is the hook paragraph, the link and one next step, about 50 words: the Explorable does the explaining. It grows toward 120 words only to carry an insight of its own, such as a research finding or poll counts.
4. **Plain words.** Facts and numbers carry the weight; no hype words and no praise adjectives.
5. **Emoji only as link markers**: ▶️ before a video, 👉 before any other link.
6. **All links in the body.** LinkedIn previews the first link, so it is the one that should get the card: the video when there is one.
7. **Organizer as a placeholder** `@<name>`: the name from [Tag names](#tag-names), else from the Organizer registry in `src/content-store/schemas/fields.ts`.
8. **Numbers carry their n** and describe only the people counted.

## Learned rules

- Hook and its context share the first paragraph, so the preview shows both. (2026-10-28-cyberforum)
- Thank the Organizer plainly: "für die Einladung und die Organisation". (2026-10-28-cyberforum)
- Up to 5 hashtags at the end: the topic from the record's `tags` second to last, `#AIEnablement` last. (2026-10-28-cyberforum, context-window)
- The hook names the concrete symptom the reader knows ("forgets the middle"), not an idiom ("goes off the rails"). (context-window)
- An offering post says who the Explorable serves: teams using it as a training tool for AI enablement, for technical and non-technical audiences alike. (context-window)
- Every post names AI enablement once in its text, best in the next step or call to action ("in your next AI enablement session"). (context-window)
