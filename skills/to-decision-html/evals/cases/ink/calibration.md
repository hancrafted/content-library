# Calibration answers

My answers to anything you would ask before building. Where a question is not answered here, proceed and mark the value you chose as assumed.

- **Mode:** `decide`. I pick; nothing is implemented from this page.
- **Audience:** me, the site's author. I designed the Episode cards and judge motion by feel. I know CSS and canvas drawing, not fluid simulation.
- **Items:** propose your own. One item must compare the three neighbouring-card approaches in `handoff.md` as its Options, without dropping any of them.
- **Depth:** `both` on the neighbouring-card item, `simple` on every other item.
- **Fidelity:** `mock` for every item. Use real card content, and show each approach's ink at a few pointer positions: far, near, and between two cards.
- **Recommendation:** recommend one Option per item and say why. I still make the pick.
- **Allowed Next actions:** "Prototype the picked approach", "Revise this page", "Stop here".
- **Gates:** approval is my explicit pick plus a Next action. Approving does not authorise production changes.
- **Design guidance:** none supplied. Use a restrained, readable style with light and dark themes.
- **Vocabulary:** use the terms in `handoff.md` as written: Episode card, magnetic ink, pool, anchor, droplet, activation distance.
- **Motion library:** none named.
- **Feedback route:** your default, at the destination the run instructions give.
