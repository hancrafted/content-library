# Agentic AI

Agentic AI is handing work to an AI and staying responsible for the result: delegating a task, breaking it into steps, clarifying what is wanted and checking what comes back.

The person stays accountable for what the AI produces, so the skill is in the hand-off and the review, and the risk is in skipping the check. Whether a whole organisation turns this into productivity is AI adoption; what the AI is given to work from is context engineering.
