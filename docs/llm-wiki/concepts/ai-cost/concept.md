# AI cost

AI cost is what running AI costs and what drives that cost: the tokens read and written, the length of the context and the price of the model chosen.

Most AI services charge by the token, so a longer context or a larger model costs more on every call. Seeing where the spend goes is the first step to controlling it. A company's costs in general belong elsewhere.
