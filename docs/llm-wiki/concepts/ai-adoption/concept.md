# AI adoption

AI adoption is whether a person or an organisation turns using AI into productivity that can be measured: the gap between what AI promises and what it delivers, and the change in how work is done that closing it needs.

Adoption depends on more than access to a tool. Work has to be reorganised around it, and much of what makes that work is tacit knowledge that people carry rather than write down. How one task is handed to an AI is agentic AI.
