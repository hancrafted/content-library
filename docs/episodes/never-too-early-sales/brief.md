# Why it's never too early to start with sales

## Format

Foundations, about 20-30 minutes: presented first to a live startup-coaching cohort, then cut as a standalone YouTube Episode.

## Audience

Early-stage startup founders and founding-team members, business and technical. Presented first to a live startup-coaching cohort, then published for YouTube reach. They know they "should sell" but treat sales as a later-stage activity; they want concrete reasons to start now and a first method.

## Style

Editorial: sober, self-explanatory Slides for a mixed audience of business and technical founders, using a consistent sticky-note/kanban motif and three-box flows.

## Learning goal

Explain why sales must start long before revenue is needed, and name the first concrete steps to build a sales pipeline.

## Actionables

1. Build a simple month-by-month cashflow view and find your zero-cash month.
2. Start lead generation now: research and maintain a list of potential customers.
3. Make first contact (cold call, inbound, network) to open opportunities.
4. Track deals in a simple pipeline and measure your conversion rate and sales cycle.

## Hook

The cashflow board that goes bankrupt in March because the big revenue only lands in April.

## Red thread

Revenue arrives months after the costs that create it, so by the time you feel you need sales it is already too late — start the pipeline before you need the money.

## Analogy

The monthly cashflow sticky-note board (cash, cost, revenue), returned to three times across the talk.

## Constraints

- Reusable across talks: no dated claims.
- Works both as a live talk and a standalone YouTube Episode.
