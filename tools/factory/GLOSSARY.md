# Factory

The machinery that turns a digital task into a measurable Pipeline of Jobs; Episodes are the first product it makes. Steering: `docs/steering/factory-vision.md`. Terms that also exist in the content library (Run, Job, Gate, Score…) are generalised here by the _Factory vocabulary_ issue; until then they mean what `GLOSSARY.md` says.

## Roles

**Operator**: the person who runs Pipelines, decides at Gates and gives feedback on a Run.
_Avoid_: User, reviewer

**Process engineer**: the person who defines Pipelines and Jobs and improves them with evidence.
_Avoid_: Pipeline author, prompt engineer

**Maintainer**: the person who builds and evolves the Factory itself. Not the content library's maintainer role (`MNT`), which keeps this repo.
_Avoid_: Developer, admin
