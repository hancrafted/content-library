# Content store

The one reader of records: Episode metadata, Events, Playlists, Templates, Explorable pages and Guides, loaded, checked and served through the entry `src/content-store/store.ts`. Its schemas are in `src/content-store/schemas/`, its delivery policy (slug lists, Episode id, record routes, visibility, page catalogue) in `src/content-store/catalogue/`, and its refusals in `src/content-store/refusals.ts` (parsing and the cross-record rules, which call the others), `src/content-store/episode-refusals.ts` (one Episode), `src/content-store/event-refusals.ts` (one Event), `src/content-store/template-refusals.ts` (Templates against their composition folders), `src/content-store/explorable-page-refusals.ts` (Explorable pages against their composition folders and Explorables), `src/content-store/guide-refusals.ts` (Guides against their composition folders; `slug-kind-refusals.ts` runs these three) and `src/content-store/featured.ts` (the Featured slots).
